Each person has a role on each brand, and that role decides what they can do there. Someone can be an **Editor** on one client and a **Viewer** on another.

## Owner

The person in charge of the brand. They can do everything an Editor can, plus:

- invite people, change roles and remove access ([Invite your team](/ayuda/invitar-equipo));
- connect and disconnect the brand's social accounts ([Connect your social accounts](/ayuda/conectar-redes));
- rename the brand and change its colors in the [Brand Kit](/ayuda/kit-de-marca);
- turn on review before publishing and choose who reviews ([Turn on review](/ayuda/activar-revision)).

Owners always review when review is on. A brand can never be left without an owner.

## Editor

Runs the day-to-day work: uploads episodes, edits clips, exports, schedules and publishes them. Editors can also change the Brand Kit's logo, text style and call to action. They don't need a Facebook account: posts go out with the connection the owner set up.

When review is on, the owner can mark an editor as a reviewer too.

## Reviewer

Meant for the agency's client. Reviewers watch clips and approve them or request changes before they go out, but they can't edit or schedule. See [Approve or request changes](/ayuda/aprobar-o-pedir-cambios).

## Viewer

Sees the brand's work (episodes, clips, calendar, posts and metrics) without changing anything. Good for someone who just needs to follow along.

## Supplier

For a content owner you have a deal with. You can invite someone with this role, but their screen isn't built yet: for now it gives no access to the workspace.

## Which role to give

1. Your editing team: **Editor**.
2. The client who has to sign off: **Reviewer**.
3. Someone on the client side who just wants to see results: **Viewer**.
4. Anyone who needs to add people or connect accounts: **Owner**.

> **Tip:** in **Team**, below the invite form, the **What each role can do** section sums this up without leaving the page.
