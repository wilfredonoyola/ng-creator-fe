The free trial lets you try all of Clipfine with your first client before choosing a plan.

## What's included

- No card required.
- Every feature: AI-picked moments, the editor, Brand Kit, review, calendar, metrics and the app.
- It covers a single brand that you own. Other brands show up when their owner invites you.
- On the pricing page, the trial is listed as {{prueba.dias}} days with up to {{prueba.horas}} video hours.

## How to start

1. Click **Start free trial** on the pricing page and create your account. See [Create your account](/ayuda/crear-cuenta).
2. Create your client's brand. See [Create a brand](/ayuda/crear-marca).
3. Upload your first episode: [Your first episode](/ayuda/primer-episodio).

## How much you've used

While you're on the trial, **Home** and **Episodes** on the web show a notice with how much you've used, for example **Free trial: 1 of 2 episodes**, next to a **See plans** link.

- Deleting an episode doesn't give the slot back.
- Already-edited clips you upload separately don't count toward the trial. See [Upload edited clips](/ayuda/subir-clips-editados).

## When you reach the limit

The notice changes to **You've reached the trial limit** and you can't upload new episodes. Everything you've already made stays. To keep going, click **See plans** and pick the one that fits: [Plans and video hours](/ayuda/planes-y-horas).

> **Tip:** run the trial with a real client episode, so you see the clips with their Brand Kit and can show them the result.
