The editor is where you get each clip ready for the client. It works like CapCut, so if your team has used it before there's nothing new to learn. It's available on the web and in the app.

## How it's laid out

- **Top**: the clip's title, save status, **Undo** and **Redo**, the quality badge, and the buttons to download or schedule.
- **Middle**: the video, previewing how the clip will look.
- **Timeline**: the **Video**, **Text**, **Captions**, **Images** and **Audio** tracks. Tap or drag to move through the clip.
- **Tools**: **Edit**, **Text**, **Captions**, **Overlay**, **Audio**, **Format** and **Auto-frame**. On the web they sit on the left; in the app, at the bottom.

## Adjust the segment and cut

1. Open a clip with **Edit** from the episode.
2. In **Edit**, drag the segment's edges to make it longer or shorter, or use **Start here** and **End here** at the current point.
3. To remove something from the middle, click **Split** at the playhead, pick the piece on the timeline and click **Remove segment**. Striped parts don't go out; the ✕ puts them back.
4. **Remove long silences** marks silences as cuts automatically. Review them one by one.
5. You can also cut from the transcript: pick the first and last word of what you want gone and remove it from the clip.

**Edit** also has **Speed**, from 0.5x to 2x (in the app it's its own tool). You see right away how long the clip will be.

## Saving and undo

There's no save button: every change saves on its own and you'll see **Saved** at the top. If your connection drops, it retries by itself. **Undo** keeps the last 100 steps (on the web, also with Ctrl+Z or ⌘Z).

## Working as a team

From the top bar you can **Take it** so the team knows you're the one editing, and **Release** it when someone else will finish it.

For everything else, see [Text and captions](/ayuda/textos-y-subtitulos), [Audio and music](/ayuda/audio-y-musica), [Clips from podcasts with several people](/ayuda/podcast-varias-personas) and [Export and download](/ayuda/exportar).

> **Tip:** use **Preview as TikTok, Reels or Shorts** to check that your text isn't hidden behind the network's buttons before you schedule.
