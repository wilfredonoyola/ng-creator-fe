You can tag the people in each clip: the show's regular cast and an episode's guests. You save them once, and when you schedule you just tap who to tag.

## The brand's cast

These are the people who are always on (hosts, regulars). Go to **Connected networks** and, under **Cast · people to tag**, tap **Add person**. Enter their name and their username on each network. One network is enough, and you can paste the profile link.

## An episode's guests

If someone came for just one show, add them on the episode page, under **Guests on this episode**. You can also add them while scheduling, with **+ Guest**.

## When you schedule a clip

In **Schedule**, the **Tag** section shows the cast and the guests. The episode's guests come already selected; the cast doesn't. Tap to select or unselect.

Each network allows it differently:

- **Instagram**: they're tagged on the Reel. You can also invite up to 3 people as **collaborators**: if they accept, the Reel also shows on their profile.
- **TikTok, YouTube and Facebook**: they don't offer tags to apps like Clipfine, so it goes as an **@mention** at the end of the description. You see it in **How it looks on each network** before scheduling.

If someone has no username on a network, they're not mentioned there.

> **Tip:** Instagram allows up to 20 tagged people per post.
