Before you can schedule clips, each brand needs its accounts connected. Every brand has its own: what you connect for one client never mixes with another.

Today you can connect **TikTok**, **YouTube**, **Instagram** and **Facebook**. Publishing goes through Upload-Post, a service that takes care of uploading the video to each network.

## How to connect a network

1. Pick the brand in the sidebar.
2. Go to **Connected networks**.
3. Under **Publishing (via Upload-Post)**, find the network marked **Not connected** and click **Connect** (for example, **Connect TikTok**).
4. Upload-Post opens: sign in to the network with the client's account and accept the permissions.
5. When you're done you're back in Clipfine and the account shows up with its name and photo.

Repeat for each network you want to use. Only the brand owner can connect and disconnect accounts, and it's done on the web. In the app, if a network is missing, it tells you to connect it on the web.

## Disconnect an account

In **Connected networks**, click **Disconnect** on the network. Careful: anything scheduled on that network will fail when it's due. If you're switching accounts, connect the new one and check the [Calendar](/ayuda/programar-y-calendario).

## If an account stops working

Sometimes a network cuts off access (for example, if the client changes their password or removes permissions). Posts scheduled on that account will fail until you disconnect it and connect it again in **Connected networks**. More in [Failed posts](/ayuda/publicacion-fallida).

> **Tip:** before clicking **Connect**, check which brand is selected in the sidebar. The account goes to that brand, and with several clients it's easy to connect one client's account to another's brand. See [Working with several brands](/ayuda/varias-marcas).
