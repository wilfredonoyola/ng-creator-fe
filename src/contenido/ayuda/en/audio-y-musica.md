The editor's **Audio** tool lets you adjust the video's voice, add background music and drop in sound effects. It's available on the web and in the app.

## The video's voice

1. Pick the **Audio** tool.
2. Under **Video voice**, move the **Voice volume** slider (up to 400%) or click **Mute**.

On the web you hear the change in the preview without exporting.

## Background music

1. Under **Background music**, open the library and listen to the tracks.
2. Click **Use** on the one you want. Later you can **Change** it or **Remove the music**.
3. Adjust the **Music volume**.
4. Turn on **Lower when people talk** so the music drops on its own while someone is speaking and comes back in the pauses.

## Sound effects

1. Move the playhead to where the effect should go.
2. Under **Effects**, click **Add** on the one you want.
3. Move it on the timeline and adjust its volume.

You can add up to 20 effects per clip.

## Your sounds

The library has Clipfine's sounds and the brand's own. To add the client's sounds, click **Upload sound** under **Your sounds**: MP3, WAV or M4A, music up to 15 minutes and 60 MB, or effects up to 30 seconds. They're saved to the brand, so the whole team can use them in their clips.

Use royalty-free music or music licensed for social media. Otherwise the network may mute or take down the clip.

## Speed

To speed up or slow down the clip, on the web go to the **Edit** tool and find **Speed**; in the app it's its own **Speed** tool. It goes from 0.5x to 2x and you see right away how long the clip will be. The voice keeps its pitch and the music isn't sped up. Text, cuts and captions are edited the same way.

More tools in [The clip editor](/ayuda/editor-basico).

> **Tip:** if a clip runs past the ideal length for a network, try 1.1x or 1.2x before cutting content. It's barely noticeable and the clip fits.
