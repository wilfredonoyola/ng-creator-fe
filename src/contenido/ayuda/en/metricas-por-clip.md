Metrics show how every clip published from Clipfine did on social, brand by brand, on the web and in the app.

## The brand summary

1. Pick the brand (the client) you want to look at in the menu. Metrics always belong to one brand.
2. Go to **Metrics**.
3. Choose the period: **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**. Numbers are compared with the previous period of the same length.

You'll see **Views**, **Interactions**, **Engagement rate**, **Followers gained** and **Clips posted**, a breakdown per network, **Top-performing clips** and **Lowest-performing clips**. Further down:

- **What works**: average views by the kind of moment the AI picked.
- **AI vs. reality**: average views by the score the AI gave each clip.
- **Best time to post**: average views by the hour each clip went out.

## One clip in detail

1. Tap a clip in the **Metrics** lists, or open the episode and tap **Metrics** on the card of a clip that's already been posted.
2. Under **How it did on social** you get the totals (likes, comments, shares, saves), **On each network**, **How views grew** and, where the network reports it, TikTok retention, how much was watched on YouTube, where people came from and countries.
3. You also see the score the AI gave the clip next to the brand average, plus **View post** to open it on the network.
4. Tap **Refresh** to read the metrics again. Anything read in the last 10 minutes isn't re-read.

## When the numbers show up

The first reading arrives about an hour after posting. After that it's read again at 6 h, 24 h, 2 days and so on, up to 90 days. Facebook only reports views, likes and comments; a "—" means that network doesn't provide the figure.

Owners, editors, reviewers and viewers of the brand can see metrics. See [Roles and permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos).

> **Tip:** to send the numbers to your client, use the PDF report. See [Reports and exporting](/ayuda/reportes-y-exportar).
