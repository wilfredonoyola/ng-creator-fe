The Clipfine app for iPhone and Android lets you keep up with your clients' work from your phone, with the same account and everything in sync with the web.

## Getting started

1. Create your account and your first brand on the web. The app has no sign-up: you can only log in with an existing account. See [Create your account](/ayuda/crear-cuenta).
2. Get the app. The last step of the welcome flow on the web shows a QR code: scan it with your phone's camera.
3. Log in with the same email and password you use on the web. You'll see every brand you work on.

## What's in each tab

- **Home**: the latest episode, its highlighted moments and the clips you're working on.
- **Projects**: your **Episodes** and your **Uploaded clips**. From there you can add already-edited clips with **Upload clips from gallery**.
- **Calendar**: what's scheduled for the brand.
- **Metrics**: the brand summary, each clip's details and **Export**. See [Metrics per clip](/ayuda/metricas-por-clip).
- **Profile**: **Work on** another brand, **Team**, Brand Kit, **Publishing defaults**, **Review before publishing**, **Notifications**, language and **Delete my account**.

## What you can do from your phone

- Edit a clip: trim, captions and text, music and sound effects, speed. See [The editor](/ayuda/editor-basico).
- Schedule to your connected networks. See [Scheduling and calendar](/ayuda/programar-y-calendario).
- Approve or request changes, with text or a voice note. See [Approve or request changes](/ayuda/aprobar-o-pedir-cambios).
- Save the finished clip to your phone's photo library.
- Get alerts on your phone. See [Notifications](/ayuda/notificaciones).

Long episodes are uploaded from the web. Plans are also bought and managed on the web: the app doesn't sell plans.

> **Tip:** if your client approves the clips, invite them as a Reviewer and ask them to get the app: they'll get the alerts and can approve from their phone.
