Review makes sure nothing goes out on a client's networks until someone approves it. It's set per brand, so you can have it on for one client and off for another.

## How to turn it on

1. Pick the brand in the sidebar and go to **Team**.
2. Under **Review before publishing**, turn on **Review clips before they go out**.
3. Under **If the time comes and nobody reviewed it**, choose **Don't publish** or **Publish anyway** (see [Overdue review](/ayuda/revision-vencida)).
4. Click **Save**.

Only the brand owner can change these settings. In the app they're under **Profile**, in **Review**.

## Who reviews

- The brand **owner** always reviews.
- Anyone with the **Reviewer** role always reviews. This role is meant for a client who only approves: they see their brand's clips and approve or request changes, but they can't edit.
- An **Editor** can also review if you mark them with **Reviews** in the team list. Useful for a team lead.

One approval is enough for the clip to go out. More on each role in [Roles and permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos).

## What changes when you schedule

When an editor schedules a clip, it stays **To review** and doesn't go out until a reviewer approves it. Reviewers get a notification. If the person scheduling is already a reviewer, the clip is approved automatically: nobody reviews themselves.

If someone who isn't a reviewer edits a clip that was already approved, it goes back to review and its posts are held again. That way what goes out is exactly what was approved.

If you turn review off, anything that was held and is still on time goes out as scheduled. Anything already overdue keeps waiting for the owner to approve it.

> **Tip:** if the owner is the only reviewer on a brand, everything lands on that one person. To share the load, invite the client as a Reviewer from [Invite your team](/ayuda/invitar-equipo).
