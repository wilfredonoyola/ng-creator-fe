Upload the full video of an episode and Clipfine transcribes it automatically and suggests the best moments for clips.

## Upload it

1. Pick the client's brand under **Working on**, at the top of the menu. The episode goes into that brand.
2. On **Home**, click **Upload episode**, or open **Episodes** and click **Upload an episode**.
3. Choose the file: mp4, mov, webm or mkv, up to 50 GB.
4. Wait for the upload. You can **Pause** and **Resume**. If your connection drops, the upload picks up on its own when it's back; if you close the tab, go back to **Episodes** and choose the same file: it continues where it left off.

## What happens next

You don't need to keep the page open. The **Episodes** list shows the status:

1. **Processing in Bunny**: the video is being prepared for playback.
2. **Transcribing**: everything that's said is turned into text.
3. **Finding moments**: AI reads the episode and picks the moments that work on their own. It takes a couple of minutes.
4. **Suggested clips**: done. You get a **Clips ready to edit** notification.

## See the clips

1. Open the episode from **Episodes**.
2. Under **Clips** you'll see each moment with its time range, hook and score. Click **Listen** to hear it.
3. Click **Edit** on the one you like to open it in the editor. See [The clip editor](/ayuda/editor-basico).
4. If you want a moment the AI didn't pick, click **+ Create clip**, mark the start and end, and go to the editor.

If the AI finds no moments, or you want a new batch, use **Find moments again**. The list of suggestions is replaced.

During the [free trial](/ayuda/prueba-gratis), every episode you upload counts, even if you delete it later.

> **Tip:** you can also bring a live stream straight from Restream instead of uploading the file. See [Import an episode](/ayuda/importar-episodio) and [How the AI picks moments](/ayuda/como-elige-la-ia).
