Images let you put the client's product, a logo or a cut-out person on top of the clip, without opening another program. It's available on the web and in the app.

## Add an image

1. In the editor, open the **Overlay** tool.
2. Tap **Image** and pick a JPG, PNG or WebP file up to 15 MB. On the web you can also drop it on the panel or right on the preview.
3. You can add up to 3 images per clip. Each one shows up on the **Overlay** track of the timeline.

## Cut it out and turn it into a sticker

1. Select the image and turn on **Remove background**. The AI keeps only the person or object; while it works you'll see **Removing background…**.
2. If it fails, tap **Retry**.
3. Turn on **White outline** to add a white border around it, like a sticker, so it stands out from the video.

## Place it and choose when it shows

1. On the web, drag it on the preview to move it and use the corner to resize it, or adjust the **Size**. In the app, move it with your finger and pinch to resize.
2. Under **When**, choose **Whole clip** or the second it appears and the second it leaves. You can also drag its edges on the timeline. In the app you choose between **Whole clip**, **First 3 s** and **First 5 s**.
3. To remove it, tap **Delete this image** (in the app, **Delete**).

Images are burned into the MP4 that gets published and the one you download. To export it, see [Export and download](/ayuda/exportar).

> **Tip:** wait until the background removal finishes before exporting. If you export while it's still processing, the image may come out without the cut-out.
