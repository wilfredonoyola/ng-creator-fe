From **Metrics** you can get a brand's numbers in the format you need: a PDF for your client, a CSV for spreadsheets or a Markdown summary.

## On the web

1. Pick the brand in the menu and go to **Metrics**.
2. Choose the period (**7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**). Whatever you export covers that period.
3. Click **Export** and choose:
   - **CSV**: for Excel or Google Sheets.
   - **Markdown**: for Notion, Slack or an email.
   - **Copy summary (Markdown)**: copies it so you can paste it straight into a message.
   - **PDF**: opens the client report in a new tab.
4. In the report, click **Download PDF** and save it as a PDF from the print window. The **CSV** and **Markdown** buttons are there too.

## What the report includes

The **Performance report** shows the brand name and date range, the headline numbers with their change versus the previous period, an **On each network** table, **Top-performing clips** and **What works** (the kind of moment that performs best and the best time to post). These are the numbers each network reports: a "—" means that network doesn't provide the figure.

## In the app

1. Open the **Metrics** tab and choose the period.
2. Tap **Export**.
3. Choose **PDF** (it opens on the web, ready to save as a PDF), **CSV** or **Markdown**, and share it from your phone.

> **Tip:** if you manage several clients, switch brands in the menu and repeat the report for each one. Every report only includes that brand's clips.
