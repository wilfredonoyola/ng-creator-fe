Clipfine tells each brand's team what's happening with its clips, so nobody has to keep checking.

## What you get notified about

- **Clips ready to edit**: AI finished finding the moments in an episode.
- **Someone took a clip**: so no one edits the same clip twice.
- **Someone scheduled a clip**: what's going out, on which networks and when.
- **A clip was published**: with the link to each network.
- **A clip is waiting for review**: only if you're a reviewer on the brand.
- **A clip you sent was approved** and **Changes were requested on a clip you sent**.
- **A review is due soon** and **A review is overdue**.

Notifications go to the brand's whole team, except whoever took the action. Review notifications go to reviewers or to whoever sent the clip. See [Turn on review](/ayuda/activar-revision).

## See them

On the web, click the bell in the menu: it shows notifications for the active brand and the **Unread** ones. From there you can **Mark all as read**. The app has a bell too.

## Choose what to get and where

1. Go to **Profile** and find **Notifications** (on the web you can also get there from the bell, with **Choose which notifications to get and where**).
2. For each notification, turn each channel on or off: **In the app**, **On your phone** and **By email**.
3. It saves automatically and applies to all your brands.

By default, everything arrives in the app and on your phone. By email you only get the ones that matter even when you're not looking: clips ready, clip published and the review ones, except clip approved.

## Phone notifications

They arrive if you have the [app](/ayuda/app-movil) installed and allowed it to notify you. If you haven't, the app's **Notifications** screen shows **Allow notifications**; if you turned them off, **Open Settings** takes you there to turn them on.

> **Tip:** if you manage many clients, keep only **Clips ready to edit** and **A clip is waiting for review** by email. Follow the rest from the bell without flooding your inbox.
