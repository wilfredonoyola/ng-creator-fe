When a brand has [review turned on](/ayuda/activar-revision), every clip an editor schedules waits for your OK. This is what reviewers do, on the web and in the app.

## Find what needs review

You get a notification when someone sends a clip for review (see [Notifications](/ayuda/notificaciones)). You'll also find it in the **Calendar**: the **To review** list shows up at the top, and the **To review** filter shows only those posts. In the app, open it from the notice in the **Calendar**.

## Approve

1. Click **Review** on the clip.
2. Watch it with **Watch the video**.
3. Click **Approve**. The clip goes out at its scheduled time, on every network that was picked.

One approval is enough.

## Request changes

1. In the same panel, write what needs to change, or click **Record voice** and say it.
2. If you recorded, check the **Transcript**: it's generated automatically and you can fix it.
3. Click **Request changes**.

You need either a written comment or a voice note. We let the person who sent the clip know, and it's marked **Changes requested**: it won't go out even when its time comes. If you just want to leave feedback without holding anything, use **Comment**.

## If you're the editor

1. Open the notification or the clip and read or listen to the comment in the history.
2. Fix the clip in the editor.
3. Go back to the review panel and click **Send for review again**. We let the reviewers know.

Note: if you edit a clip that was already approved and you're not a reviewer, it goes back to review on its own. What goes out is always what was approved.

> **Tip:** voice notes save your client time. Ten seconds of talking explains more than a paragraph, and the editor can still read the transcript.
