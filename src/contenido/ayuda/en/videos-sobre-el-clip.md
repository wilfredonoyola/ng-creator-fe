Videos let you put B-roll, a reaction or the product in motion on top of the clip, just like images. For now it's on the web.

## Add a video

1. In the editor, open the **Overlay** tool.
2. Under **Videos**, tap **Video** and pick an MP4, MOV or WebM file up to 200 MB and 2 minutes. You can also drop it on the panel or on the preview.
3. You can add up to 3 videos per clip. If the video is vertical, it goes **Full screen** and covers the whole clip.

## Place it and choose when it shows

1. When it's not full screen, drag it on the preview to move it and use the corner to resize it, or adjust the **Size**.
2. Under **When**, choose the second it appears and the second it leaves, or drag its edges on the **Overlay** track of the timeline. It doesn't loop: it leaves when the video ends.
3. Under **Starts at**, choose which second of the video it starts from.

## With or without sound

- **No sound**: it shows, but you don't hear it.
- **Mixed**: you hear it together with the clip's voice, at its **Volume**.
- **Video only**: while it shows, the clip's voice is muted. Music and effects keep playing.

If the video has no audio, the panel tells you and it can't be mixed.

Videos are burned into the MP4 that gets published and the one you download, over the images and under the texts and captions. To export it, see [Export and download](/ayuda/exportar).

> **Tip:** a MOV recorded on an iPhone may not show in the preview on some browsers. It still shows in the exported video.
