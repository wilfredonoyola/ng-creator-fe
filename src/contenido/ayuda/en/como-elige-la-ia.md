Once an episode is transcribed, the AI reads the transcript and suggests the moments that work as clips on their own. Your team starts by choosing, not by watching 90 minutes of video.

## What it looks for

- Moments that make sense without having seen the rest of the episode.
- A hook in the first few seconds and a clear ending.
- A length between 15 and 90 seconds.
- If the moment is an answer, the clip starts at the question.

It leaves out anything that isn't content: promotions and giveaways, requests to subscribe or like, show announcements, the intro and goodbye, and songs or jingles. If nothing is worth it, it would rather suggest nothing than a weak clip.

## What each moment shows

- A **score** from 0 to 100: how likely it is to work on social media.
- A **reason**: **Strong hook**, **Hot take**, **Complete story**, **Quotable line**, **Humor**, **Emotion** or **Surprising fact**.
- A short title and, sometimes, a **hook**: on-screen text for the first few seconds.

It suggests up to 15 moments per episode, sorted by score. Everything is a suggestion: you can change the segment, title and hook in the editor.

## How to use them

1. Open the episode from **Episodes**, or from **Top moments (AI)** on the home screen.
2. Click **Show what's said** to read the moment, or play it.
3. Click **Edit** to open it in the [editor](/ayuda/editor-basico).

If you want a fresh batch, the episode has **Find moments again** (it replaces the suggested list).

## Create a clip by hand

If the client asks for a specific moment, click **+ Create clip** on the episode: find the moment in the transcript, mark the start and end, and go to the editor. In the app, you type a few words of what's said and it finds the spot. A clip can be up to 3 minutes long. Clips made this way are labeled **Made by hand**.

## Several clips at once

To get several clips out of one video (a webinar, for example), click **✨ Several clips** on the episode or in **Content**:

- **Ask the AI**: write what you want, like "one clip per topic", "the practical tips" or "audience questions with their answers". The AI looks for those moments across the whole video and shows them in order, with their time range and why they match what you asked for. Check the ones you want (you can change their titles) and click **Create**. If a moment is already a clip, it tells you.
- **Paste the time ranges**: one per line, like `0:05:10 - 0:06:20 How to set prices`. The title is optional: if you leave it out, the AI writes one.

You can create up to 20 at a time, each up to 3 minutes long and adjusted so no word gets cut. They're yours under **Made by hand**, ready to edit. You can also ask for them from [ChatGPT or Claude](/ayuda/conectar-chatgpt-claude).

It works the same on the web and in the app.

> **Tip:** use the reason to explain to your client why you picked each clip. It makes approval in [review](/ayuda/aprobar-o-pedir-cambios) easier.
