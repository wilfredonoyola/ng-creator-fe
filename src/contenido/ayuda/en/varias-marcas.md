With one account you can work on several brands, one per client, and switch between them in a click.

## What stays separate

Each brand is its own workspace. It has its own episodes, clips, calendar, posts, metrics, connected accounts, [Brand Kit](/ayuda/kit-de-marca) and team. Nothing mixes between clients: whatever you upload or schedule stays in the brand you had selected at the time.

Your role is per brand too. You can be **Owner** on one and **Editor** or **Reviewer** on another. See [Roles and permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos).

## Switch brands on the web

1. At the top of the left menu you'll see **Working on**, with the active brand's name and color.
2. Click it to open the list of your brands.
3. Pick the one you want. The active brand has a check mark.

The color strip on the left of the switcher changes with each brand, so you can tell where you are at a glance. If the menu is collapsed, click the brand's logo to expand it and switch.

## Switch brands in the app

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Under **Brand** you'll see all your brands: tap the client's. The selected one is highlighted.

## How brands get to your account

- When you [create your account](/ayuda/crear-cuenta), a brand is created in your name, with the free trial.
- The others show up when each one's owner invites you. See [Invite your team](/ayuda/invitar-equipo).

## Notifications across brands

On the web, the bell shows notifications for the active brand. The [Notifications](/ayuda/notificaciones) preferences you set in **Profile** apply to all your brands.

> **Tip:** before uploading an episode or scheduling a clip, glance at **Working on**. It's the simplest way to avoid posting to another client's account.
