An episode is a client's long video (a podcast, an interview, a live stream) that Clipfine turns into clips. It goes into the brand you have selected, so check which client you're working on before you import.

## Upload the video from your computer

1. On the web, pick the client's brand and go to **Episodes**.
2. Click **Upload an episode** and choose the file: mp4, mov, webm or mkv, up to 50 GB.
3. Wait for the upload to finish. You can **Pause** and **Resume** it; if your connection drops, it picks up on its own when you're back online.
4. If you close the tab halfway, go back to **Episodes** and pick the same file: it continues where it left off.

Episodes are uploaded from the web. In the app you can follow their progress and work on their clips.

## Bring in a live from Restream

1. The brand owner goes to **Episodes** and clicks **Connect Restream**. Each brand has its own connection, so one client's lives never mix with another's.
2. Open **Bring in a live from Restream**: you'll see your finished lives with their length.
3. Click **Import** on the one you want. There's no need to wait for YouTube to process the video or to keep the page open.

Restream only keeps recordings for a limited time: next to each live you'll see how many days are left before Restream deletes it. You can also bring in a live from the app, in **Projects**, once the brand has Restream connected.

## What happens next

The episode moves along on its own: processing, transcribing, finding moments and finally a number of suggested clips. If something fails, a **Retry** button shows up on the episode's row. See [How the AI picks clips](/ayuda/como-elige-la-ia) for what comes next.

Episodes use hours from your plan; see [Plans and hours](/ayuda/planes-y-horas). If the clip is already edited, you don't need an episode: use [Upload edited clips](/ayuda/subir-clips-editados).

> **Tip:** if you imported a Restream live and the clips don't look sharp enough, the episode has **Re-import from Restream**, which brings in the original recording and keeps your clips. Export them again afterwards.
