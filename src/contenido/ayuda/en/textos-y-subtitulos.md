Text and captions are what make a clip work with the sound off. In the editor you handle them with the **Text**, **Captions** and **Overlay** tools, on the web and in the app.

## Add text

1. Pick the **Text** tool and click **Add text** to choose a design: **Impact**, **Black box**, **Brand box**, **Yellow**, **Shadow** or **Clean**.
2. Type your text and tap the words you want highlighted.
3. Drag the text on the video to move it. Under **When** you choose whether it shows for the **Whole clip**, the **First 3 s**, or a specific range; you can also stretch its edges on the timeline.

## Text style

In **Text**, the **Text style** section has ready-made styles such as **Pop**, **Karaoke**, **Pill**, **Beast**, **Brand**, **Editorial**, **Lower third**, **Minimal** and **Keynote**. The style sets the font, colors and boxes for text and captions; the content, position, size and timing are still up to you. Each client's default style is chosen once in the [Brand kit](/ayuda/kit-de-marca).

## Automatic captions

1. Pick the **Captions** tool and turn on **Show captions**. They come from the transcript, word by word.
2. Drag the caption in the preview to move it up or down; the dot on the right makes it bigger.
3. With the **Karaoke** style you can also choose **Font**, **Effect**, **Position** and **ALL CAPS**. With other styles, the style sets those. To switch, click **Change the style**.

## Fix a mistranscribed word

1. In **Captions**, click **Fix it in the transcript**.
2. Tap the word, type the right one and click **Save**. You can also **Remove from caption** or go **Back to original**.

A fix changes the caption, not the clip's timing.

## Images and branding

With **Overlay** you add images (JPG, PNG or WebP): drag to move, use the corner to resize. If the brand has a logo and call to action in its [Brand kit](/ayuda/kit-de-marca), they show up on the timeline automatically.

> **Tip:** to keep captions from being covered by TikTok or Reels buttons, check the clip with **Preview as TikTok, Reels or Shorts** before scheduling it.
