Exporting builds the clip's final MP4 with all your changes. You need it to download the clip; to schedule it on social networks you don't: it exports on its own.

## On the web

1. At the top right of the editor, click **Download**. If the MP4 is up to date, it downloads right away. If not, it exports and downloads on its own once it's done.
2. The small arrow next to **Download** opens **Video options**. There you can see the MP4's status and click **Export video** or **Export again**.
3. To schedule it, click **Schedule**. If the MP4 is missing or outdated, Clipfine exports it before publishing.

## In the app

1. Tap **Done** at the top right of the editor. **Your clip** opens.
2. Choose **Schedule on socials** or **Download MP4**. The download is saved to Photos; if the video is still exporting, it's saved once the export finishes.

## What each status means

- **Not exported**: there's no MP4 yet.
- **Exporting**: it takes a minute or two. You can move to another screen or another clip; it keeps exporting in the cloud.
- **MP4 ready**: it has all your changes.
- **Changes not exported**: you edited after exporting and the MP4 doesn't include those changes. Export again if you're going to download it.

If the export fails, click **Try again**.

## Mark it ready for the team

In **Video options**, under **For the team**, click **Mark ready**. The clip moves to the ready-to-schedule list and the whole team sees it on the calendar. If it needs more work, use **Back to editing**. In the app you'll find this in the clip's menu.

To choose networks and times, see [Scheduling and calendar](/ayuda/programar-y-calendario). If the brand has review turned on, see [Turn on review](/ayuda/activar-revision).

> **Tip:** don't export every time you change something. Saving is automatic; export only when you're about to download the video.
