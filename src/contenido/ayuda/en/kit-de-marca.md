The Brand Kit is what every clip of a brand carries: its colors, the text style, the logo in a corner and, if you want, a call to action at the end. You set it up once per client and it's applied automatically.

## Set it up

1. Pick the brand under **Working on**.
2. Open **Brand Kit**: on the web it's in the menu, under **Administration**; in the app, under **Profile**.
3. Under **Brand theme**, choose the **Primary**, **Secondary**, **Text** and **Background** colors. If the brand has a logo, **Use logo colors** suggests its palette.
4. Under **Text style**, choose how the hook and captions look.
5. Under **Logo**, click **Upload logo** (PNG or JPG; we remove the background). Then choose **Where it goes**, **Size** and **Opacity**.
6. Under **Call to action at the end**, type the text (for example, "Watch the full episode on YouTube") and how many seconds it lasts.
7. Check the **How it looks (9:16)** preview and click **Save Brand Kit**.

In the preview, anything outside the dotted line gets covered by the social network's interface: keep the logo and text inside it.

## Who can change it

- The colors and the brand name: only the **Owner**.
- The text style, logo and call to action: the **Owner** and **Editors**.
- The rest of the team can see it but not change it.

In the app you can adjust everything except uploading the logo, which is done on the web.

## When it applies

New clips come out with the saved Brand Kit right away. Clips that were already processed pick it up when you process them again. In each clip's editor you can change the style and turn off the logo or the call to action for that clip only. See [Text and captions](/ayuda/textos-y-subtitulos).

> **Tip:** if you skipped it when you [created your brand](/ayuda/crear-marca), set it up before editing your first clips: it saves you from changing the style one clip at a time.
