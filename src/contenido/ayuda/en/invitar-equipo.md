Invite anyone who needs to work on a brand by email: your editing team, the client who approves clips or someone who only needs to watch.

Access is per brand: each one has its own team. Only the brand's **Owner** invites people and changes access; everyone else can see the team but not change it.

## Invite someone

1. Pick the brand under **Working on**, at the top of the menu (in the app, under **Brand** in **Profile**).
2. Open **Team**. On the web it's in the menu, under **Administration**; in the app, under **Profile**.
3. Under **Invite someone**, type their email and, if you like, a **Name (optional)**.
4. Choose a role. Below the selector you'll see what each one can do. See [Roles and permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos).
5. Click **Invite**.

If the person already has a Clipfine account, their access is ready right away. If not, they get an email with a temporary password and show up under **Invited, not signed in yet** until they sign in for the first time. You can **Cancel** the invitation from there.

## Change a role or remove access

**With access** lists the whole team, with their last sign-in.

- To change someone's role, pick a different one in their selector (in the app, **Change role**).
- To take them off the brand, click **Remove** and confirm. You can give access back by inviting them again.

A brand is never left without an owner: the last owner can't have their role changed or be removed. If you want to hand the brand over to someone else, make them **Owner** first.

## If the email doesn't arrive

The email may land in spam or junk. Ask them to look there for the Clipfine message. When they sign in with the temporary password, they'll be asked to choose their own.

> **Tip:** to add the same person to another client, switch brands under **Working on** and send the invitation again. Each brand has its own team. See [Working with several brands](/ayuda/varias-marcas).
