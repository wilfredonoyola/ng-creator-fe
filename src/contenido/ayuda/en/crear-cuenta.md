Creating an account lets you try Clipfine for free, no card needed, with 2 episodes.

## Sign up

1. Open Clipfine on the web and, on the sign-in screen, click **Create one**.
2. Fill in **Email** and **Password**. **Name (optional)** is how your team will see you in notifications and on clips.
3. Your password needs 8 or more characters, an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol. The pills under the field show what's missing.
4. Click **Create account**.
5. Check your email for a 6-digit code. Enter it and click **Confirm and sign in**.

If the code doesn't arrive, check your spam folder. You can ask for a new one with **Send another code** or go back with **Change email**.

## What comes next

The first time you sign in, Clipfine walks you through three steps:

1. **Your brand**: the name of the client or show you'll be working on. See [Create your brand](/ayuda/crear-marca).
2. **Brand Kit**: the logo and text style for your clips. You can **Skip** it and set it up later.
3. The app: a QR code to get Clipfine on your phone. You can skip this one too.

When you're done, **Go to dashboard** takes you home, ready to [upload your first episode](/ayuda/primer-episodio).

## If you were invited

If someone from an agency or a brand invited you, you don't need to sign up: you'll get an email with a temporary password. Sign in with that email and password, choose your own password and you'll see the brand you were added to. See [Invite your team](/ayuda/invitar-equipo).

> **Tip:** for now, accounts are created on the web. After that you can sign in to the [app](/ayuda/app-movil) with the same email and password.
