The most common questions from agencies using Clipfine, with short answers.

## Can I manage several clients in one account?

Yes, that's what Clipfine is built for. Each client is a brand, with its own episodes, clips, calendar, metrics and Brand Kit. You switch brands from the menu. See [Working with several brands](/ayuda/varias-marcas).

## Does one client's data mix with another's?

No. Access is per brand: each one has its own team, and someone invited to one brand doesn't see the others. Metrics and reports also always cover a single brand.

## Who sees what?

It depends on the role you give each person in each brand: **Owner**, **Editor**, **Reviewer** or **Viewer**. For example, a Reviewer approves or requests changes but doesn't edit, and a Viewer only looks. See [Roles and permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos).

## Does my client need an account?

Only if you want them to log in. To have them approve clips, invite them by email with the Reviewer role: they get an email with a temporary password and can access their brand on the web or in the app. See [Invite your team](/ayuda/invitar-equipo) and [Turn on review](/ayuda/activar-revision). If you only want to show them results, send them the PDF report: [Reports and exporting](/ayuda/reportes-y-exportar).

## Is it available in English?

The interface is in Spanish and English, on the web and in the app. Change it under **Profile**, in **Language**; **Automatic** uses your browser's or phone's language. Transcription, moments and captions are built for Spanish-language videos.

## Which networks can I post to?

TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, from the calendar. Every clip also downloads as a vertical MP4 for any other platform. See [Connect your networks](/ayuda/conectar-redes).

## Is there a clip limit?

No. Plans are measured in video hours uploaded per month; clips and exports are unlimited. See [Plans and video hours](/ayuda/planes-y-horas).

## Can I upload clips I already edited elsewhere?

Yes, and they don't use up hours. See [Upload edited clips](/ayuda/subir-clips-editados).

## Can something go out without anyone approving it?

If you turn on review for a brand, whatever the team schedules waits for a reviewer to approve it. If the time comes and it hasn't been reviewed, it doesn't go out. See [Expired review](/ayuda/revision-vencida).

## Can I cancel?

Yes. Under the Terms, you can cancel at any time with no penalty, and your plan stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for. Payments aren't refundable, except for a charge made by mistake or twice.

## What happens if I delete my account?

Under **Profile**, **Delete my account** removes your user and your access to every brand. Brands where you're the only member are archived, and their content is kept for 30 days in case you want it back. If you're the only owner of a brand where other people work, you first need to make someone else the owner in **Team**.

## Is there an app?

Yes, for iPhone and Android, with the same account as the web. See [The iPhone and Android app](/ayuda/app-movil).
