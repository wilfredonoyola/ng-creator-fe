This guide explains what happens when the scheduled time arrives and the clip is still waiting for the client's review.

## Before it goes overdue

Two hours before the scheduled time, if nobody has reviewed the clip, we notify the brand's reviewers. The notice arrives in Clipfine and, depending on your preferences, by push and email (see [Notifications](/ayuda/notificaciones)).

## When the time comes

It depends on what the owner chose in **Team**, under **If the time comes and nobody reviewed it**:

- **Don't publish** (the default): the clip doesn't go out and is marked **Overdue, not reviewed**. We notify the reviewers and the person who sent it for review.
- **Publish anyway**: the clip goes out on time and is marked **Went out unreviewed**, and a notification is sent too.

Either way, a clip with **Changes requested** never goes out: a reviewer already said no.

## Publish an overdue clip

1. Open the clip from the notification or from the **Calendar**.
2. Click **Approve**. Since the time has passed, it asks what to do with the overdue posts.
3. Choose **Publish now** to post it right away, or **Pick another time** and set a day and time that hasn't passed.

It works the same on the web and in the app.

If the brand turns review off while there are overdue clips, they don't all go out at once: they keep waiting for the owner to approve them.

> **Tip:** for clients who are slow to respond, schedule with some margin and keep **Don't publish**. A clip that goes out a day late is better than one the client never saw.
