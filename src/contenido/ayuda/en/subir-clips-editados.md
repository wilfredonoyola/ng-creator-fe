If a clip is already edited (a CapCut reel, a tip shot on a phone, a YouTube cut), you upload it as is and it joins the same flow as clips from episodes: client review, scheduling to social networks and metrics. It skips the AI and episode transcription.

## Upload them on the web

1. Pick the client's brand and go to **Posts**, **Upload clips** tab. You can also get there from **Episodes**, through the **Already have the clip edited?** link.
2. Drop your videos on **Drop your videos here** or click **Choose videos**. You can upload several at once.
3. Each clip shows **Preparing the video…** and within seconds it's **Ready to schedule**.
4. On the clip's card, click **Schedule**, or **Edit** if you want to touch it up first.

## From the app

1. Go to **Projects** and pick **Uploaded clips**.
2. Tap **Upload clips from gallery** and choose one or more videos from your phone.

## Good to know

- Formats: MP4 or MOV, up to 3 minutes each.
- They **don't use hours** from your plan. Hours are for the episodes Clipfine transcribes and cuts.
- If you don't touch it, it goes out as is. If the video is already H.264 it's published without re-compressing; if it comes in another format, like iPhone HEVC, it's converted once at high quality.
- If you open it in the editor you can add captions, the brand's style, a hook or music. The card then says **edited in Clipfine**. See [The clip editor](/ayuda/editor-basico).
- Use the filters (preparing, ready, scheduled, published) to see where each clip is.
- Only editors and brand owners can upload clips. More in [Roles and permissions](/ayuda/roles-y-permisos).

If the brand has [review turned on](/ayuda/activar-revision), uploaded clips also go through client approval before they're published.

> **Tip:** export the week's batch from your editing tool and upload it in one go. The client's calendar stays complete in one place, wherever each clip came from.
