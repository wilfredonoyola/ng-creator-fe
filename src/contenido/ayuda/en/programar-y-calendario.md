Scheduling means choosing which networks a clip goes out on, with what text and at what time. The calendar shows everything scheduled and published for each brand, on the web and in the app.

## Schedule a clip

1. Open the clip and click **Schedule** (it's in the editor and on the episode).
2. Under **Where**, select the accounts it will go out on. All of the brand's accounts start selected.
3. Under **Description**, write the text that goes below the video. The brand's hashtags are added at the end; remove the ones that don't fit this clip. For YouTube, also fill in the **YouTube title**.
4. Check **How it looks on each network**: it warns you if the text is over any network's limit.
5. If you want, pick the **Cover**. Otherwise each network sets its own.
6. Under **When**, pick the day and time, and click **Schedule on N networks**.

You don't need to export first: if the clip has changes not exported yet, it exports on its own before publishing. If the brand has [review turned on](/ayuda/activar-revision), the clip stays **To review** until it's approved.

## The calendar

Open **Calendar** from the sidebar. You'll see:

- The week, with **Previous week**, **Next week** and **Today**. Times are in the time zone shown at the top.
- How many posts are scheduled, published and failed.
- Filters by network and, if the brand uses review, **To review**.
- **Ready to schedule**: clips the team marked as ready in the editor, each with a **Schedule** button.

## Change something scheduled

A scheduled clip is edited on the same screen you scheduled it with, in **Edit schedule** mode:

- In the clip editor, next to the button, you see **Scheduled · Fri 6:00 PM · N networks**, and the button becomes **Edit schedule**.
- In the **Calendar**, click the clip and that same screen opens.

The top says when and on which networks it goes out. Everything comes loaded as scheduled: networks, text, tagged people, YouTube title, cover and date. Change anything and click **Save changes**:

1. If you change the **text**, the **cover** or the **tags**, it's updated on each network.
2. If you change the **date**, every network moves to the new time. If they went out at different times and you don't touch the date, each keeps its own.
3. If you **check a new network**, it's scheduled there.
4. If you **uncheck a network**, it's canceled there (you confirm first).

Anything that already went out on every network is view-only, with a **View on** link for each network.

> **Tip:** if a clip goes out on several networks, schedule it all at once. They share the text and time, and you can change everything together from the calendar.
