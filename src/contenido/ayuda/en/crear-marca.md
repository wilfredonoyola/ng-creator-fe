A brand is your workspace: its episodes, clips, social accounts and team all live under it. In an agency, each client is a brand.

## Create it

You create your brand in the first step after you [create your account](/ayuda/crear-cuenta):

1. In **Your brand**, type the **Brand name** (2 to 60 characters). It can be the client's name or the show's name.
2. Tell us **Where did you hear about us?**.
3. Click **Create brand**. You become the brand's **Owner**.
4. Continue to the **Brand Kit**, or click **Skip** to set it up later. See [Brand Kit](/ayuda/kit-de-marca).

Signing up creates a single brand in your name, with the free trial. Other brands you work on show up when their owner invites you. See [Working with several brands](/ayuda/varias-marcas).

## The free trial

A new brand starts on the trial: 2 episodes. Your home screen shows how many you've used. Deleting an episode doesn't give the slot back. When you hit the limit you'll see **You've reached the trial limit** with a **See plans** button. See [Free trial](/ayuda/prueba-gratis).

## Rename it

1. In the left menu, open **Brand Kit**.
2. Click **Rename**, type the new name and click **Save**.

Only the owner can rename the brand. The whole team sees the new name in the brand switcher and in notifications.

> **Tip:** use the name your team knows the client by. It's what shows under **Working on** at the top of the menu, and it helps avoid publishing to the wrong brand.
